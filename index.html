ABOUT EDLINE

Engineering experience built around water and allied works.

EDLINE TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED was established in 2001 as Edline water services to execute engineering-related projects with emphasis on water and allied civil/electro-mechanical works, construction and re-incorporated as a limited liability company in 2010.

Today, the company takes on the role of main contractor from small, medium to large-size projects and performs project management services to coordinate specialist trades for industrial/commercial projects.
